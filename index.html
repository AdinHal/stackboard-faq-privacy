Frequently Asked Questions
What is Stackboard?
Stackboard is a clipboard manager for iPhone and iPad. It automatically saves everything you copy so you can find and paste it again at any time, without having to re-copy or re-type anything.
How does it work on iOS?
Because iOS restricts background clipboard access, Stackboard uses a custom keyboard extension. Once you enable the Stackboard keyboard in your device settings, it appears alongside your regular keyboard in any app. Tap a saved clip and it pastes instantly, without leaving the app you are in.
How do I enable the Stackboard keyboard?
Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and select Stackboard. When prompted, tap Allow Full Access. This permission is required so the keyboard extension can read and save your clipboard entries to the shared app container.
Why does the keyboard need Full Access?
iOS keyboard extensions cannot communicate with the main app without Full Access enabled. Stackboard uses this permission solely to save clipboard entries on your device. No data is sent to any server unless you opt in to iCloud sync.
Does my clipboard data leave my device?
No. All clipboard history is stored locally on your device by default. iCloud sync is strictly opt in and disabled by default. If you choose to enable it, your clips are synced privately through your personal iCloud account.
Can I use Stackboard across multiple devices?
Yes. Enable iCloud sync in Stackboard settings and your clipboard history will be available across all your iOS devices signed in to the same Apple ID.
How do I pin a clip?
Long press any clip in your history to reveal options, then tap Pin. Pinned clips stay at the top of your list and are never automatically deleted.
Is there a limit to how much history Stackboard saves?
You can configure a history size limit and auto expiry in Stackboard settings. This lets you control how much storage the app uses and how long clips are retained for privacy.
Can I search my clipboard history?
Yes. Tap the search bar at the top of the Stackboard keyboard or the main app to search across your entire clipboard history instantly.
Does Stackboard work with images?
Stackboard saves text, URLs, and images that you copy to your clipboard. You can browse and re-paste any of these from your history.
How do I delete a clip?
Swipe left on any clip in the list to reveal the delete option, or long press to access additional options including delete.
Who do I contact for support?
